OUR STORY

About Literary Light

Literary Light is an independent literary magazine created to celebrate books, thoughtful reading and the people who bring stories to life.

We share literary discoveries, book coverage, author conversations and opportunities for readers and writers to connect.

Our aim is to create a welcoming space where diverse voices and meaningful stories can find an audience.

FOR AUTHORS & PUBLISHERS

Submit Your Book

We welcome enquiries from authors, publishers and literary representatives.

What you can submit

  • Books for editorial review consideration
  • Author spotlight proposals
  • Literary interview enquiries
  • Publisher and publicist introductions

Submission guidelines

Please provide accurate book details, a short description and your contact information. Submissions are considered at the editorial team's discretion.

Submitting a book does not guarantee publication or a review.

Your email application will open with your submission details ready to send.
